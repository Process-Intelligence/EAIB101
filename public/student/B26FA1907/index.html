Gross pay rules and their source (Labor Law of Mongolia):
• Standard working day = 8 hours, standard working week = 40 hours — Article 70.1–70.2.
• Night hours = 22:00–06:00 — Article 72.1.
• Overtime worked on an ordinary weekday, or on a weekly rest day, with no substitute day off given: at least 1.5× average compensation — Article 53.1.
• Work on a public holiday with no substitute day off given: at least 2.0× average compensation — Article 52.1.
• Work during night hours with no equivalent rest given: extra compensation on top of the hour's normal rate — Article 54.1. The law leaves the exact rate to the collective agreement/contract of employment; this calculator uses a commonly cited practical rate of +0.2× (i.e. 1.2× total) as a default assumption. Confirm the actual contractual rate for real payroll use.

2026 tax/SI assumptions (unchanged from the original calculator): employee social insurance = 9.5%, with a monthly contribution base ceiling of ₮7,920,000. Employee health insurance = 2%, uncapped. Both are computed on the new total gross pay above (base pay + overtime + rest-day + holiday + night premium + bonus), not on the base salary alone. Health insurance is included in taxable income and net pay but is not shown as its own output line. Income tax brackets and credits are annual; the calculator annualizes monthly taxable income, computes the annual tax and credit, then converts back to a monthly amount.
