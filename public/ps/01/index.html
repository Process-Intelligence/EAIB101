Problem Set 01

Salary, Tax and Insurance

Read the law first. Then let the machine write the code.

Нээлттэй One session Research → Build → Compare prompts

The task

Every payslip in Mongolia carries the same two deductions, and almost nobody who receives one can explain them. Today you find out how they actually work — by reading the law, not by asking a model — and then build a page that calculates them correctly for any salary.

Where to look

legalinfo.mn mta.gov.mn ndaatgal.mn Any free AI Any text editor

Алхмууд

Step 1

Find these in the law, before you open any AI tool

  • Which two laws govern this. Find both on legalinfo.mn and write down their full names
  • The employee's social insurance contribution rate — and, separately, what the employer pays
  • The upper limit on the monthly salary used to calculate contributions
  • Every income tax band and the rate that applies to each
  • The monthly tax credit: how much, and who is entitled to it
  • Whether social insurance is subtracted from salary before income tax is calculated, or after
  • The date each rule takes effect — some amendments are passed in one year and start in another

Do not ask AI for the rates. It will confidently give you numbers that were correct in some other year.

Step 2

Build it

What your calculator must do

The page

  • One input: gross monthly salary in tögrög
  • Six output lines, each labelled: social insurance withheld, taxable income, tax before credit, credit applied, income tax withheld, net pay
  • Contributions stop rising above the ceiling
  • The credit is applied after tax is calculated, and tax never goes below zero
  • Recalculates as you type; thousand separators, no decimals
  • One .html file that opens by double-click with no internet

Five test cases — calculate these by hand first

  • A salary low enough that income tax comes to zero
  • An ordinary salary in the middle of the range
  • A salary sitting exactly on a band boundary
  • A salary above the contribution ceiling
  • A salary of zero
  • Your page must show all five and match your hand calculation exactly

Accepted only if

  • Every rate in your code carries a comment naming the law, the article, and the date you checked it
  • All five hand calculations match the page, to the tögrög
  • You can say in one sentence why insurance is subtracted before tax — or why it is not
  • Nothing in the file was accepted from AI without you checking it against the law yourself

If the page and your hand calculation disagree, one of them is wrong. Find out which before you submit.

Step 3

The same job, two prompts

Run both. Then write down the difference.

Prompt A

Make an HTML page that calculates
Mongolian salary tax and social
insurance.

Watch for

  • Where did its rates come from?
  • Did it name a year, an article, a source?
  • Did it subtract insurance before tax?
  • Did it handle the ceiling at all?
  • Would you sign your name under it?

Prompt B — fill the brackets with what you found

Write a single-file HTML calculator. No libraries, no CDN.

Rules I verified myself on legalinfo.mn on [date]:
- Employee social insurance: [rate]% of gross salary
- Contributions stop above [amount] MNT per month
- Taxable income = gross salary - employee social insurance
- Income tax bands: [every band and its rate]
- Monthly tax credit: [amount] MNT, applied after tax is computed
- Tax may never be negative

Build:
- One input: gross monthly salary
- A table showing: insurance, taxable income, tax before credit,
  credit, income tax, net pay
- Thousand separators, no decimals, recalculate on every keystroke
- A test section showing these cases: [your five cases]

Use only the rates above. Invent nothing. If anything needed is
missing, stop and ask me instead of guessing.

Then write the conclusion — six sentences, no more

  • Which numbers did Prompt A invent, and how did you catch it?
  • Which requirement did A silently skip that B got right?
  • How long did each version take to make correct?
  • What in Prompt B did the work — name the one line that mattered most.

Step 4

Make it a real payslip

Now the salary itself is a calculation

Nobody is paid a flat monthly figure. Days worked, overtime, night hours and holidays all decide what the gross number actually is — and every one of them changes the tax and the insurance underneath it.

Find in the labour law

  • The standard working day and week, and the norm used to turn a monthly salary into an hourly rate
  • The premium for overtime, and whether it may be given as time off instead of pay
  • The premium for work at night, and for work on a rest day or a public holiday
  • What happens when two of these overlap — do the premiums stack, and in what order
  • Which of these payments social insurance and income tax actually apply to

Add to your page

  • Days worked, and days in the working month
  • Hours worked, and hours of overtime
  • Night hours, rest-day hours, public-holiday hours
  • Bonus or additional pay, entered as its own field
  • A gross pay breakdown, line by line, before any deduction — then the same six deduction lines as before, recalculated from the new gross

Prove you did not break what already worked

  • Full month, no overtime, no night hours: all five original cases must return exactly the same numbers as before
  • Add three new hand calculations — half a month, one overtime hour at night, a full day on a public holiday
  • Every new multiplier carries a comment naming the article it came from
  • Extend Prompt B; do not start a new chat. In two sentences, say what the model broke when you added the new rules

Adding a parameter is easy. Adding one without breaking the five things that already worked is the actual skill.
